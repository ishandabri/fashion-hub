# Product Requirements Document

## Status

Draft foundation. Product requirements will be populated and refined as context is provided.

## Product

**Fashion Hub** — the specific audience, problem, and product promise are not yet defined.

## Product hypothesis

Fashion Hub will help a defined audience discover, organize, evaluate, or act on fashion-related content in a focused and visually clear experience. This hypothesis must be validated and replaced with the user’s product context.

## Goals

- Define the primary user and their most important job to be done.
- Establish a focused MVP with measurable outcomes.
- Create a clear, minimal, accessible interface using shadcn/ui.

## Non-goals

- No feature scope is committed until the product context is supplied.
- No assumptions about commerce, social features, personalization, or inventory should be implemented as requirements.

## Success metrics

To be defined. Candidate categories include activation, task completion, discovery engagement, retention, and conversion—depending on the final product direction.

## Experience requirements

- Light, minimalistic visual direction.
- shadcn/ui component foundation.
- Use the shadcn icon approach consistently.
- Responsive, keyboard-accessible, and usable on touch devices.
- Explicit loading, empty, error, and success states.
